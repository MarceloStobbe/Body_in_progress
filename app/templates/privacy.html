Privacy Policy

How your training data, preferences, and local information are secured.

1. Local Data Storage & Session State

Body in Progress (BIP) operates securely on your browser. All your custom workout logs, profile parameters, configuration states, and media preferences are maintained locally using localStorage. Your active session state is seamlessly preserved across all page transitions.

2. User Preferences & Themes

Your visual preferences (such as Light/Dark theme settings, accent colors, and module opt-in toggles like the Training Playlist & Audio Hub configuration) are saved locally to ensure a consistent UI/UX experience without tracking your identity.

3. Third-Party Media & Audio Streams

When utilizing the Training Playlist & Audio Hub feature, direct audio streams (radio feeds) are loaded via HTML5 audio elements directly from their respective public broadcasters, utilizing standard network requests handled by third-party content delivery networks (CDNs).

4. Data Security & Control

You retain total ownership and control over your data. You can clear or reset your stored application configurations or sign out at any time directly through the application interface or your browser settings.
