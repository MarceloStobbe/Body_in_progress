BMI Calculation & Reference: Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated as your weight in kilograms divided by the square of your height in meters. It serves as a standard screening tool to categorize body weight status (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese) based on general population metrics.

Note: BMI is a general reference standard. Your ideal body composition depends heavily on muscle mass gains, strength training regimen, and individual body structure.
